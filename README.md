# adidas_sales_analysis
This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard and data analysis for Adidas retail sales data. The goal is to uncover actionable insights that can improve decision-making across product categories, regions, sales channels, and customer demographics.
📊 Key Objectives
Analyze sales performance across different product categories, genders, and sales methods.

Identify top-performing regions and cities based on profit and revenue.

Evaluate the effectiveness of sales methods (In-store, Outlet, Online).

Compare monthly sales trends to discover seasonal patterns.

Provide profitability insights across different retail partners.

📌 Dashboard Highlights
Product Category Sales Distribution
Understand which Adidas products drive the most revenue.

Sales Trend Over Time
Analyze monthly trends to identify growth or dips.

Top Performing Cities by Profit
Get a quick view of where Adidas performs best geographically.

Sales Method Effectiveness
Compare in-store, outlet, and online sales performance.

Units Sold by Gender & Product
Learn how product performance varies by gender.

Profit by Retailer
Analyze contributions from major partners like Amazon, Walmart, and West Gear.

📁 Tools Used
Power BI – Dashboard creation & visual storytelling

Excel / CSV – Data cleaning & transformation

Python (optional) – For preprocessing or exploratory data analysis

✅ Outcome
The case study helped identify:

Men’s streetwear and athletic footwear as the highest revenue drivers

In-store purchases outperforming other sales methods

Key cities (Charleston, New York, San Francisco) that consistently drive high profit
